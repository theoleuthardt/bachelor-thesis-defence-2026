Reveal.js keyboard shortcuts
=============================

Navigation
  N, Space, →, ↓      Next slide / fragment
  P, ←, ↑             Previous slide / fragment
  Home                First slide
  End                 Last slide
  Page Down           Next slide (skip fragments)
  Page Up             Previous slide (skip fragments)

Overview & display
  Esc, O              Toggle slide overview (grid view)
  F                    Toggle fullscreen
  S                    Open speaker notes window
  B, .                Pause presentation (black screen)
  Alt + click          Zoom into clicked element

Other
  ?                    Show this help as an on-screen overlay
